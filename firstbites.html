⚠️ Evidence-Based Allergen Introduction Rules
  • One at a time: Introduce only 1 new major allergen at breakfast or lunch.
  • 15-Minute Rule: Offer ¼ tsp mixed into a safe puree, wait 15–20 minutes, then complete the serving.
  • Sustained Exposure: Once introduced safely, offer each allergen 1–2 times every week to build and maintain immune tolerance.
