🚨 Emergency Safety Cheatsheet

😮 Gagging (Normal Reflex)

Loud & Red = Learning to Chew

  • Airway is open
  • Coughing, spluttering, retching
  • Red face, watery eyes, tongue push
👉 Action: Stay calm. Sit on hands. DO NOT finger-sweep or slap back (it pushes food into airway). Let baby cough it up.
🛑 Choking (Airway Blocked)

Silent & Blue = Immediate Emergency

  • Airway is blocked
  • Silent, unable to cry or vocalize
  • High-pitched wheezing, wide panicked eyes
  • Lips or nails turning dusky/blue
🚨 Action: Yell for 911. Flip baby head-down along forearm: 5 firm back blows between shoulder blades, flip to 5 chest thrusts.

⚠️ The Allergen Maintenance Protocol

Passing an allergen once is step one. To lock in permanent tolerance, offer each cleared allergen 1–2 times every week. The tracker below monitors when each allergen was last fed.

🗺️ Master Feeding & Milestone Roadmap

7m → 18m

From first tastes to family table foods. Use this guide to time meal additions, texture progressions, and the 1-year milk transition.

Phase 1

7 to 8 Months: Skill Building & Allergen Clearance

1–2 Meals/Day

Primary Goal: Oral motor mapping (tongue lateralization) and introducing all Top 9 allergens.

🗓️ Week-by-Week Action Plan:

  • 7m W1: 1 meal/day. Iron-rich base foods + Peanut protocol (thinned butter in puree).
  • 7m W2: Scrambled egg strips + steamed vegetable spears (sweet potato, zucchini).
  • 7m W3: Move to 2 meals/day (Breakfast + Dinner). Introduce whole-milk Greek yogurt.
  • 7m W4: Introduce Whole Wheat (toast crust strip) + Lentils / Beef crumbles.
  • 8m W1–2: Tree nut butters & Tofu strips. Practice resistive chewing (clean mango pit).
  • 8m W3–4: Flaked wild salmon & Sesame tahini. Establish 1–2x/week maintenance cadence.
⏰ Ideal Daily Flow: 7:00 AM Milk (7 oz) → 8:00 AM Breakfast → 11:30 AM Milk → Nap → 2:30 PM Milk → 5:30 PM Dinner → 6:30 PM Milk → Bedtime.
Phase 2

9 to 11 Months: The Pincer Grasp & Table Foods

3 Meals + 1 Snack

Primary Goal: Transition from palmar palm-raking to thumb-index finger pincer grasp. Move to 3 full meals.

✂️ Texture Shift:

Switch from long spears to ½-inch soft cubes (steamed carrot coins, ripe banana dices, shredded soft chicken).

🥄 Utensil Practice:

Offer short, thick silicone spoons pre-loaded with yogurt or mash for baby to self-navigate.

⏰ Daily Routine: 3 solid meals (Breakfast 8 AM, Lunch 11:30 AM, Dinner 5:30 PM) + 4 milk feeds (20–28 oz total) + open cup water practice at meals.
Phase 3

12 to 14 Months: The 1-Year Milk & Cup Shift

3 Meals + 2 Snacks

Primary Goal: Solids become primary nutrition. Drop bottles and switch to open/straw cups.

  • Cap Whole Milk at 16 oz/day: Drinking more than 16–20 oz of cow's milk can cause milk-induced iron deficiency anemia.
  • Drop Baby Bottles: Transition all milk and water into open or weighted straw cups.
  • Adopt 3+2 Cadence: Breakfast (7:30), Snack (10:00), Lunch (12:30), Snack (3:30), Dinner (6:00).
Phase 4 & Safety

15 to 18 Months: Toddler Table & Choking Guardrails

100% Family Food

Division of Responsibility: You decide what, when, and where to serve; your toddler decides whether and how much to eat.

🛑 Mandatory Choking Modifications Until Age 4:

  • Grapes & Cherry Tomatoes: Quarter lengthwise (never round discs).
  • Hot Dogs / Sausages: Slice lengthwise into quarters, then dice.
  • Whole Nuts & Sticky Nut Butter: Keep finely ground or thinned into purees.
  • Raw Apples & Carrots: Steam soft, grate finely, or bake.
  • Popcorn & Hard Candies: Strictly avoid.
